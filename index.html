My Skin Story

Throuout my adolescence I never had any issues with my skin. It was not until I turned 20 years old that evrything changed My acne started to worsen really rapidly. What began as a few occasional breakouts quickly escalated into something much harder to manage.

Like many people, my first instinct was to buy well-known skincare products I had seen recommended online. They helped a little bit, but the situation escalated so fast that the damage became really hard to deal with. The acne and the post-marks it left behind took a real toll on my self-esteem. It was one of the hardest periods I have gone through.

⚠We tend to want to solve things ourselves and listen to influencers on the internet, but it makes you spend a lot of money in vain and does not solve the problem. The best decision I ever made was going to see a dermatologist.

Here's what my dermatologist prescribed

My dermatologist prescribed me a treatment plan that finally worked. In about 6 to 7 months, my skin was unrecognizable, so healthy and clear that even I could not believe it! I was prescribed tretinoin, a potent retinoid that accelerates skin cell turnover and unclogs pores, alongside tablets for hormonal acne. These tablets work by blocking androgen hormones in the body, which reduces excess sebum (oil) production and decreases inflammation, ultimately rebalancing the skin from within.

Together, they sped up my recovery significantly, though it still took several months of consistency and patience to achieve and mantain the clear, healthy skin I have today."

⚠These medications were prescribed specifically for me by a dermatologist. Please do not self-prescribe. Always consult a professional before starting any prescription treatment!

⚠ These products work for me personally, but results may vary — what works for one person may not work for another. This is simply my own experience and a reminder that consistency, patience, and the right professional guidance can make all the difference

Tips That Worked For Me

✨ Never skip SPF — sun exposure makes acne marks darker and last longer.

✨ Change your pillowcase at least twice a week — it collects oil and bacteria overnight.

✨ Do not pick or pop pimples — it causes scarring and spreads bacteria.

✨ See a dermatologist — do not try to solve it alone!

✨ Introduce new products one at a time so you can tell what is working.
